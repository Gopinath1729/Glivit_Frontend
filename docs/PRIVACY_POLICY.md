## What GLIVT processes

GLIVT processes account and organization identifiers needed to authenticate users and isolate each organization's fleet data. Registered GPS devices provide latitude, longitude, time, and any optional device-health fields configured by the device. GLIVT uses these records to provide live vehicle locations, route history and playback, distance, motion status, geofence events, speed alerts, operational reports, and GPS-device connectivity health.

With the user's permission, the Android app may access the user's precise foreground location to display the user relative to tracked vehicles and to help select a geofence center. The Android app does not track the phone in the background and does not use the phone as a vehicle GPS device.
